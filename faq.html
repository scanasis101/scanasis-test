Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions teams ask before briefing a measured survey or BIM project.

What services does Scanasis provide?

Scanasis provides 3D modelling, 2D CAD drawings, topographical modelling and drawings, and digital twin-related services using client-supplied survey data and project information.

Can you work with existing point clouds and survey data?

Yes. We use point clouds and survey data supplied by clients or their survey partners to create the required 3D models and 2D CAD drawings.

What file formats can you deliver?

We deliver files in formats suited to the agreed project requirements. The required formats are confirmed during the project briefing.

What information do you need to start a project?

A brief outlining the project scope, required outputs, available survey data, and any specific requirements is enough to get started. Existing drawings or reference files can also help.

What determines the level of detail in a 3D model?

The required level of detail depends on the model’s intended use and project requirements. We agree on the scope and level of detail before work begins.

Do you provide topographical drawings and digital twin services?

Yes. We provide topographical drawings and modelling, along with digital twin-related services, based on the agreed project scope and available data.

How do you get started with Scanasis?

Contact us with your project brief, required outputs, and available survey data. We will review your requirements and discuss the next steps.

How can I contact Scanasis?

You can contact us through our website’s contact page or email info@scanasis.com to discuss your project requirements.
